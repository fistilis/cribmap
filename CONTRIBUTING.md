# Contributing to Cribmap

Thanks for looking!

- **Bugs and ideas:** open an issue. Include your VTT, browser, and the encounter file if you can (strip anything private).
- **Pull requests:** keep them small. Run `node tools/validate.mjs examples/*.json` before you submit.
- **Format changes:** propose them in an issue first. The format is meant to work in more than one VTT.
- **Content:** example encounters must be original or SRD-only. Don't include text, names or maps from published adventures.
- **Sign-off:** please add `Signed-off-by` to your commits (`git commit -s`) to certify the [Developer Certificate of Origin](https://developercertificate.org/).

By contributing, you agree your contribution is licensed under Apache-2.0.
